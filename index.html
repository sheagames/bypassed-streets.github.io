How to play

Left thumb Move · drive · ride (push to the edge to sprint)

Right thumb Look around · two fingers: camera in / out

Punch Hold for a haymaker

Jump Over a railing · Brake when driving (hold and steer: drift)

Block · Kick · Dodge Fight back

E Interact · get in / out · steal · take the helm · Pick up / Eat food

Shoo Holding food: wave off a bin chicken winding up to lunge

Weapons Opens the weapon wheel: tap Thongs, Bat or Boomerang · Throw / Swing hold to wind up, let go to throw / swing (the bat: tap again after a hit for the backhand, again for the overhead) · Drop put it down · walk up to one on the ground and tap Pick up

Privacy

Chats with NPCs are processed by AI services and logged for up to 30 days to improve the game. Don't type personal info. Bot check by Cloudflare Turnstile: Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Play stats: a random ID kept in your browser, with load time, play time, device type, screen size and which missions you finish. Kept up to 180 days.

Crash reports: if the game stops without closing properly, your next visit sends the readings from its last couple of minutes: browser and device type, screen size, frame rate, graphics and memory readings, and where you were on the game's map (not your real location). Kept 30 days.

Both go only to the game's own server, to find what breaks. No names, no accounts, no IP addresses stored, nothing you type or say, no advertisers or third-party trackers.

Contains violence and strong language.

Untick to stop both. Off by itself when your browser asks sites not to track (Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control). A crash report that isn't sent stays on this device: see Report a problem.
